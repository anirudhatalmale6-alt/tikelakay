  • Refund: the ticket stops working at the door, the place goes back on sale, the unpaid commission is cancelled. A commission already paid out is shown "to recover", never hidden.
  • The Ajan's Share button now gives the flyer link with his code (e.tikelakay.com/e/…?ref=CODE): WhatsApp shows the flyer, and a buyer from that link earns him the 30%.
